About

I recently graduated from the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at New York University, where I completed the accelerated Bachelor's Master's track. I am now a Member of Technical Staff at Cohere, where I work on agentic and LLM evaluations.

I am very fortunate to be advised by Professor Eunsol Choi. I am a member of the ML2 Lab, affiliated with CILVR@NYU. My interests include machine learning and natural language processing. Currently, I am researching agentic search and retrieval systems.
